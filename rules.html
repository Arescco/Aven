Official Server Rules

🛡️ In-Game Gameplay Rules

  • No Hacking or Unfair Modifications: The use of hacked clients, X-Ray packs, auto-clickers, macros, or inventory modifications that provide an advantage is strictly forbidden.
  • No Exploiting or Bug Abuse: If you discover a glitch or duplication bug in the server mechanics, you must report it to the staff immediately. Exploiting bugs will result in a permanent data wipe.
  • No Griefing or Stealing: Players are not allowed to destroy, modify, or steal from another player’s builds unless explicitly permitted by specific world gamemode rules.
  • No Server Lag Machines: Building redstone clocks or entity loops designed intentionally to drop the server's TPS or crash the proxy network is a bannable offense.

💬 Chat & Community Conduct Rules

  • Zero Tolerance for Hate Speech: Discrimination, racial slurs, homophobia, and severe harassment will result in an immediate network-wide mute or ban.
  • No Chat Spamming or Text Walls: Sending consecutive duplicate messages, spamming symbols, or writing in all capital letters disrupts public chat rooms and is not allowed.
  • No Advertising: Do not share IP addresses, server invite links, or URLs for other Minecraft networks, YouTube channels, or Discord servers.
  • Keep Chat English-Only: To allow our global staff team to monitor chat rooms effectively and keep players safe, please stick to English in public channels.

⚖️ Account & Network Security Rules

  • No Ban Evading: If you are punished on one account, logging in on an alternative account (ALT) to bypass your mute or ban will result in a permanent IP ban.
  • No In-Game Scamming: Tricking players out of hard-earned in-game items or currency during survival or economy trading sequences is strictly forbidden.
  • Your Account is Your Responsibility: Staff will not reverse punishments if your sibling, friend, or a hacker gets your account banned. Secure your Minecraft credentials!
